- Emit each (src, rel, dst) once.
- For every fire named in a moratorium bulletin emit: one TRIGGERED edge (Fire -> its moratorium), one ISSUED edge

Mention conventions (the resolver keys on these exactly):
- Fire: `<Name> Fire <year>`, e.g. `Eaton Fire 2025`.
- Moratorium: one per fire, `<Name> Fire moratorium <declaration date>`, e.g. `Eaton Fire moratorium 2025-01-07`.
- Regulation: bill number as `SB 824` or `AB 2996`.
- Official: full personal name as printed, without title, e.g. `Ricardo Lara`.
- ZIP: the five digits.
