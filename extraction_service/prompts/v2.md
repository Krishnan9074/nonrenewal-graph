- Regulation: bill number as `SB 824` or `AB 2996`; otherwise the regulation's name as printed, e.g.
  `Sustainable Insurance Strategy`, `Net Cost of Reinsurance in Ratemaking Regulation`.

- Insurer: the company name as printed, e.g. `State Farm General Insurance Company`, `Mercury Insurance`, `CSAA`.
- Official: full personal name as printed, without title, e.g. `Ricardo Lara`, `Gavin Newsom`.
